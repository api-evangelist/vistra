Fund managers often know when a transfer agent relationship is no longer working. Acting on it can be a different matter. Errors, omissions, delays and miscommunications produce investor dissatisfaction, advisor frustration and missed deadlines, which are the very problems you hoped to avoid by outsourcing your back office.
